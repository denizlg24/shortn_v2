## Findings that change the plans

1. **Stored click totals are ~9× the recorded events.** Links: `Σ clicks.total` = **163,280** vs **17,605** click events (1,582 of 2,671 links mismatch). QR: **26,230** stored vs **13,431** scan events (235 mismatches). Historic events were lost (pre-V3 collections, the deletion bug, the `save()` race). → `legacyTotal` preservation in M7 is mandatory, and dashboards must show `legacyTotal + events since cutover` for all-time totals while time-series charts only cover recorded events. The README's "2.4M clicks" claim must be re-verified before reuse in marketing.
2. **275 QR codes have no exact backing link** under the rule `{urlCode: qr.urlId, isQrCode: true, qrCodeId}`, yet every QR's `urlId` matches exactly one link (0 ambiguous, 0 duplicates). These are the legacy `/qr/{code}` codes. → M4 matches on `{urlCode: qr.urlId}` (unambiguous because duplicates are 0) and records whether the backing link carries the QR flags; scans of these codes may currently be recorded as `type: "click"` on the link — the redirect golden suite must capture their current attribution before P2.
3. No orphan clicks, no campaign drift, no QR destination drift, no Pinata-heavy assets (3 URLs) → M12 is tiny.
